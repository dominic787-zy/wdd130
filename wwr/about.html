Whitewater Splash Rafting Co.

At Whitewater Splash, our mission is to deliver unforgettable adventures on the river while keeping safety and fun paddling side by side. We believe every rapid tells a story, and we're here to help you write yours.

History

Whitewater Splash Rafting Co. was founded in 1998 by a small group of river guides who wanted to share their love of the water with the world. What started as a single raft and a dream has grown into one of the region's most trusted rafting outfitters, guiding thousands of adventurers safely down some of the most thrilling rapids in the country. Over the decades, we've built our reputation on expert guides, top-notch equipment, and a genuine passion for the river.
